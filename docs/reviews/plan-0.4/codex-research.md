## Questions we should not have asked

- **Q1's direction was already answered publicly.** The [maintainer's #181 reply](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/pull/181#issuecomment-6137467644) says EmPoWer on L1 followed by a bridge to LEZ. A polite follow-up can narrow the request to the **current 0.3.1 LEZ recipient encoding, channel, and a tested end-to-end example**.
- **Q2 and Q3 remain legitimate release questions.** Draft #935 code answers what exists on that branch, not what 0.4 will ship.

## New facts that change our plan

1. **Funding discovery is more concrete than Stage R says.** There is a [documented L1 faucet](https://github.com/logos-co/roadmap/blob/v5/content/testnets/logos-node-operator-guide.md#fund-the-node-for-consensus), a [Blockchain-app Channel Deposit wizard](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-docs/blob/main/docs/blockchain/node-app/bridge-assets-from-logos-blockchain-to-zone-using-app.md#L22-L47), [EmPoWer module methods](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain-module/blob/b2a19e46987cd96902ef5fbe6c6f2a78f26aa6cf/blockchain_module.lidl#L43-L49), and the [v0.3.1 LEZ metadata decoder](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/v0.3.1/lez/sequencer/core/src/lib.rs#L173-L176). Stage R should test this path before treating team-funded treasury transfers as the only way forward. Its claim that the new genesis has no funded test accounts conflicts with [v0.3.1's funded-account source](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/v0.3.1/lez/testnet_initial_state/src/lib.rs#L159-L174); live balances still need checking.
2. **#935's token/ATA/AMM genesis is not a safe release assumption.** Its [author notes ATA was removed on `dev`](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/935#discussion_r4255196023), while [#935 retains it](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/testnet_initial_state/src/lib.rs#L189-L196). Resolve the program provenance and IDs before Stage F3.
3. **The L1 may have survived the LEZ reset.** The [2026-10-13 L1 0.3.2 checklist](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/issues/3804) leaves new-genesis steps unchecked. Treat survival as **INFERRED** until an old L1 balance or genesis ID is checked; do not copy LEZ's reset conclusion to L1.
4. **0.4 proof UX needs an early fee spike.** Open [#949](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/949) says private transactions pay fees inside the proof, quotes expire after **20 blocks**, public dry-run RPCs are off by default, and only **four** private transactions fit a block at its current cap. These draft constraints can break a 5–8-minute claim flow and belong in F1, not after product construction.
5. **#181 is still unmerged.** The [PR](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/pull/181) removes the faucet criterion only if accepted; the plan must keep the current criterion in its coverage audit until then.
