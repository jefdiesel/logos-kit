# Independent review of the Logos Kit 0.4 plan

Checked 2026-10-14 against the local plan and code, read-only Git/GitHub sources, the current `lambda-prize` master criteria at `ad608bf93f`, and read-only live RPC calls. **VERIFIED** means observed in the cited source or command; **INFERRED** is a reasoned consequence; **UNKNOWN** is an unresolved release or runtime fact. Draft PR behavior is evidence about that draft, not a promise about testnet 0.4.

## Verdict

The two-line strategy serves the stated goal of restoring a usable wallet now while building the full token, NFT, proof and Market product for 0.4. The plan nevertheless promotes several draft-actor behaviors into product commitments, and its gate freshness, private-fee timing and prize-criterion coverage need proof before implementation hardens. Keep the combined launch and full scope, but add the protocol decision gates and explicit criterion evidence below, and advance the SDK work needed by independent builders.

## Top 10 findings, ranked by impact

1. **Private fees may make the proposed claim and proof flow impossible under the draft expiry rule — INFERRED.** Open [LEZ #949](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/949) moves private fees inside the proof, gives a quote a **20-block** window, limits a block to four private transactions, and disables public dry-run RPC by default. The project's measured real-proof runs took 469–757 seconds ([`docs/dev/perf.md:14-19`](../../dev/perf.md)); at the plan's roughly ten-second blocks, the quote window is about 200 seconds. The exact quote acquisition point and refresh mechanism remain **UNKNOWN**, so the mismatch is a risk rather than a proven impossibility. Add an F1 **real-proof fee/expiry spike** before F0 claim UX or N4: quote → prove → submit → expiry/requote, with measured fee, retry, cancellation and low-memory behavior; do not promise a 5–8-minute queued claim until it succeeds.

2. **The proposed gate does not yet establish the required off-chain freshness semantics — INFERRED.** [LP-0001 §Functionality and §Reliability](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0001.md#L38-L46) requires verification *without submitting a transaction* and a new proof to invalidate the previous proof for that token [at lines 69–70](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0001.md#L65-L70). [`PLAN-0.4.md:163-180`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) says a self-transfer/notification makes stale/refresh inherent, but a proof only checked off-chain need not mutate chain state; #935's [token messages](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/programs/token/core/src/lib.rs#L18-L53) also lack `AssertHolding`. Make F1(a,b) a protocol decision: define when a proof is **issued** and the vault state changes, prove an off-chain verifier reads the authoritative latest state, then test proof A valid, proof B issued for the same token without consuming A on-chain, A stale, B valid, transfer and expiry. Include master and printed NFTs, two copies from one collection, and two different contexts. Preserve the separate proof-only session and shared on/off-chain verdict table from [`PLAN-LP0001.md:189-238`](../../dev/PLAN-LP0001.md).

3. **The token-program dependency is not a release contract; the private receive address model can strand assets — VERIFIED draft conflict / INFERRED product risk.** v0.3.1 [#926](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/926) removed token/ATA/AMM from genesis; draft [#935 genesis](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/testnet_initial_state/src/lib.rs#L189-L196) retains actor variants, while its [author says ATA was removed on `dev`](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/935#discussion_r4255196023). Its [private token cell](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/programs/token/src/transfer.rs#L30-L70) holds one descriptor and rejects a mismatched deposit; reviewers identify an [unclaimable pending-message path](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/935#discussion_r4254886371) and [empty-cell occupation](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/935#discussion_r4260569659). Replace the assumed ATA default and reusable private receive code in F0/F3/N2 with a versioned **per-asset destination and binding** decision, tested for wrong asset, occupied cell, duplicate delivery, recovery and refund. Gate F3 on the final reference token program's source, ID and deployment path; test capability discovery when a program appears or disappears while the wallet is installed, so the UI and CLI switch on/off without reinstalling.

4. **Current prize text still requires the faucet, and adoption is not an exit proof — VERIFIED.** Current [LP-0021 lines 28–76](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0021.md#L28-L76) still requires an SDK faucet mini-app plus 10 independent Basecamp UI apps, 150 distinct-account on-chain testimonials over two months, and 30 Discord/30 X vouches; [#181](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/pull/181) is open. [LP-0001 lines 95–102](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0001.md#L95-L102) adds its conditional 50-testimonial baseline, five independent collections, three independent NFT/proof apps, 100 gated actions over two months, and 20/20 vouches. Stages C/L in [`PLAN-0.4.md:191-198`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) say only “criteria audit” and user-run promotion. Add a criterion ledger with evidence URL/count/month/independence fields and an explicit **post-launch qualification stage**. Keep the owner's one combined product launch and promotion decision; make the adoption work visible as later required work, and make the SDK usable early enough for genuine independent commit histories.

5. **Approval UX lacks the prize's hard requirements for actor calls — VERIFIED gap.** [LP-0021 lines 42–45](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0021.md#L42-L45) requires a human-readable summary of assets and approvals that may move, called-program source-verification status, and estimated/used gas if 0.4 exposes it; [LP-0001 lines 56–60](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/blob/ad608bf93f/prizes/LP-0001.md#L56-L60) adds specific-asset versus collection-wide approval and separate proof/account permissions. [`PLAN-0.4.md:107-139`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) details activity but F0/N3 do not specify a trusted interpretation of declared actors, nested Sync/Async messages, authorization or source verification. Add an F1 actor-effects/registry/gas spike, then an N3 approval acceptance test with a malicious or unknown program, unlimited approval, nested asset movement, ambiguous simulation and proof-only grant. Block signing when effects cannot be rendered safely; show “unknown effects” rather than inventing a reassuring summary.

6. **Stage R treats funding as an external blocker more strongly than its sources justify — VERIFIED correction / UNKNOWN live outcome.** The v0.3.1 [genesis source](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/v0.3.1/lez/testnet_initial_state/src/lib.rs#L77-L91) publishes new account keys and [funded accounts](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/v0.3.1/lez/testnet_initial_state/src/lib.rs#L159-L174), contradicting the blanket “new genesis doesn't fund test keys” in [`PLAN-0.4.md:8-14`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md). A [maintainer says](https://github.com/logos-co/lambda-prize/pull/181#issuecomment-6137467644) the intended route is EmPoWer on L1 then bridge, and Logos publishes a [Channel Deposit guide](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-docs/blob/main/docs/blockchain/node-app/bridge-assets-from-logos-blockchain-to-zone-using-app.md#L18-L89), though its version is 0.2.1. The live balances and working 0.3.1 bridge remain **UNKNOWN**. In R, read the balances of published genesis accounts and exercise L1 faucet/EmPoWer → bridge with a tiny deposit to a separately controlled key; record the v0.3.1 Borsh recipient metadata and final LEZ balance, then seek a team treasury transfer if those fail. Do not use published shared genesis keys as a production drip treasury.

7. **Incoming → Claim assumes a sender display and a recoverable queue that the draft does not guarantee — VERIFIED draft behavior / INFERRED UX risk.** #935 describes blinded senders, and its [review](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/pull/935#discussion_r4255034738) notes a sealed publication lacks sender data; [`PLAN-0.4.md:149-158`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) promises “sender as plain text.” Draft wallet [pending/claim](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/wallet/src/lib.rs#L660-L743) and [restore depth](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/wallet/src/lib.rs#L1542-L1618) need product integration. Show sender only if authenticated data exists. Give each receipt durable states (detected, proving, submitted, claimed, recoverable failure), idempotent retry, queue position, resource estimate and a recovery scan for old installs and restart/OOM mid-proof; test a pending receipt that cannot claim because its destination was occupied. The one-HD-node-per-holding design also needs a measured restore-depth limit and a test that keys remain discoverable after many empty or rejected destinations.

8. **The AMM example does not prove an atomic NFT Market — INFERRED.** #935's [AMM swap](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-execution-zone/blob/93e6cbf9814b4ce94e7ded56bfe1e2518f1245b8/lez/programs/amm/src/swap.rs#L10-L80) is a vault/payout pattern, not proof that an NFT listing owns the unique item or that a private deferred payout and its claim cannot be lost. [`PLAN-LP0001.md:240-249`](../../dev/PLAN-LP0001.md) promises a losing racing buyer pays nothing, while #949 changes public and private fee semantics. Require F1(c) to establish listing custody, buy/cancel mutual exclusion, asset and payment atomicity or explicit deferred-settlement states, failed-buy fee disclosure, refund/recovery and proof invalidation on list/sale. Record executable adversarial tests before the M implementation; retain the requested Market scope.

9. **Stage X needs a transaction state model, not one generic failed detail sheet — INFERRED.** Current [`engine.rs:90-139`](../../../crates/wallet-engine/src/engine.rs), [`TxRow.qml:18-24`](../../../modules/logos_kit_wallet_ui/qml/LogosKitWallet/TxRow.qml) and [`ProofView.qml:145-175`](../../../modules/logos_kit_wallet_ui/qml/LogosKitWallet/ProofView.qml) explain the present gaps, but [`PLAN-0.4.md:107-139`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) copies Glow's fee-paid failure language across transactions. Official [Phantom history guidance](https://help.phantom.com/articles/view-your-wallet-history-in-phantom-35862137120019), [detail/explorer guidance](https://help.phantom.com/articles/45592795487123), and [pending/retry guidance](https://help.phantom.com/articles/5985106844435) distinguish a pending transaction from a failure and advise checking chain history before retry. [Rainbow](https://rainbow.me/support/extension/navigating-the-tokens-and-activity-screens) offers explorer and copy-hash actions from history, while [Family](https://family.co/support/activity) tracks both transactions and dApp approvals and [lets users hide spam](https://family.co/support/activity-options); X does not yet specify approval events or history spam controls. Specify separate **proof failed before submission**, **submitted/unknown**, **included/reverted**, **included/succeeded**, and **private claim pending** states; derive fees from actual inclusion, reconcile by hash across reset/restart, and keep the explorer link available from history/detail. The [Explorer manifest](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/lez-explorer-ui/blob/main/metadata.json#L12-L13) only permits generic app launch, so the web deep link is the reliable transaction-specific action until an open-at intent exists. Avoid assuming 0.4 has a public indexer or browser CORS.

10. **The order delays SDK adoption and actors learning behind a potentially costly interim port — INFERRED.** R is described as “cheap” in [`PLAN-0.4.md:12-19`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md), yet read-only `git apply --check` against the v0.3.1 ref failed patches 0001/0002/0004/0005, as [`status-2026-10-14.md:36-41`](../../dev/status-2026-10-14.md) also says; the upstream wallet changed substantially. [`PLAN-0.4.md:34-55`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md) puts X before F0/F1 and N3 after F3/N2, despite the 10 independent LP-0021 apps needing time-spread histories. Keep R as a bounded live-network repair and X as a first-class UX deliverable; run F1 protocol risks and the chain-neutral N3 session/grant/SDK contract work as soon as their inputs exist. Make R and X completion gates about observable wallet behavior, not a prerequisite for every actor spike. F2's testimonial actor should also have a negative test for forged nested/async calls alongside its root-author authorization and required attribution fields [`PLAN-0.4.md:171-176`](../../dev/PLAN-0.4.md); do not cut token/NFT/Market scope to save time.
