### 2.2 The 0.4 model (verified in draft PR #935, final release unknown)
- **What changes:** programs become actors handling messages (`actor_entrypoint!`, `SyncMessage`/`AsyncMessage`); there's no Plan/Apply and no `Plan::inspect`; private accounts have no identifiers; incoming private assets arrive as pending messages the wallet claims with a proof; first sends to a new private address need a recovery binding.
- **#935 status:** open at `93e6cbf`, 388 files. Its genesis still has actor ports of token, ATA and AMM, but its author says "ATA is removed on dev". So **which token program 0.4 ships, and its IDs, is unknown.** We asked on Discord.
- **Which of our designs must be redone on actors:** the gate (`Plan::inspect`), vault identifiers (`PRF(seed, i)`), the testimonial program and the Market escrow.
- **What carries over:** the UI, approvals, policy, history, Basecamp shim, LWS-0 method names, QML kit, theme, apps and docs. Semantics under the method names will change.

### 2.4 Private fees in draft PR #949: a risk for every private flow (verified draft)
- **The rules:** every private transaction pays its fee inside the proof, priced at a fee-state height that must be at most **`PRIVATE_FEE_WINDOW = 20` blocks** old when it lands. At most **4** private transactions fit in a block. Dry-run RPCs are off by default on public sequencers.
- **Why it hurts:** at ~10 s blocks the window is ~200 s, but our measured real proofs take **267–757 s** (`docs/dev/perf.md`). A laptop-proven private transaction would land after its window.
- **Upstream:** nobody had raised this on #949 or #941. We asked on Discord at 09:50.

## 5. Decisions (all made)
- **0.3.1 vs 0.4:** both, as two lines (the user left the choice to me; Codex agreed). `main` gets a bounded 0.3.1 repair with native LGO only; everything token-based goes on `next/lez-0.4`. No hosted copies of token programs on 0.3.1.

## 6. What the Codex review changed (`docs/reviews/plan-0.4/codex-review.md`, `codex-research.md`)
1. **Funding:** the new genesis is funded (§2.3). Stage R now funds our own deployer and treasury keys from the published genesis accounts, as on 2026-10-06. It never uses the shared keys as a treasury. It also tests L1 faucet / EmPoWer → bridge with a tiny deposit.
2. **#949 private fees:** a real-proof fee/expiry spike is now the first item in F1, before any claim or proof UX is promised.
3. **Gate freshness:** "a new proof invalidates the old one, verifiable off-chain without a transaction" (LP-0001) needs an explicit issuance design and a test matrix in F1 (proof A valid → proof B issued → A stale).
4. **Approvals on actors:** an F1 spike on actor effects, source verification and gas. N3 acceptance tests with malicious, unknown and nested calls. Signing is blocked when the effects can't be shown.
5. **Plan gaps fixed:**
   - per-asset private destinations instead of one reusable receive code;
   - durable claim states and no "plain-text sender" promise;
   - Market adversarial tests before building;
   - a real transaction state model in stage X;
   - the faucet criterion kept until #181 merges;
   - a criterion evidence ledger and a post-launch qualification stage (Q);
   - program source-verification status on approvals;
   - capability switch-on/off tests;
   - chain-neutral SDK contract work started early.
