### 2.2 Why the model changes (verified, Marina's thread "LEZ 0.3 → 0.4")
- 0.2: private transactions couldn't share public state.
- 0.3: Plan → Apply. Several private transactions can update the same public account, but the model is awkward to build on.
- 0.4: **programs become actors that handle messages against their own live state; the separate plan step goes away.** The code is LEZ PR #935 "feat!: transactional actors with side-effects": open, +34.9k/−29.4k lines, 388 files. It uses Cast/Call messages, encrypted private messages and blinded senders, and it **removes private account identifiers and requires ownership evidence for initialization**. Spec updates are in PR #932.
- What this breaks in our plan: the gate design (`Plan::inspect` inside a private transaction, §4.4), vault identifiers (`PRF(seed, i)`, §4.2), our testimonial program, and the market escrow, all of which must be redesigned on actors. The wallet UX, SDK API, CLI, connect/approval flows, token list, design and docs carry over.
- SPEL is focused on 0.4, docs get only minimal 0.3 updates, and the DEX won't ship on 0.3.

**How another app can open the Basecamp explorer (verified in Basecamp source, `main` at `5f8cca8`, newer than our pinned 0.3.0):** Basecamp has `basecamp.apps.launch {app}` and `basecamp://app/<name>` deep links (`app/links/LinkUrl.h`, `docs/app-to-app-intents.md`). They open an app *by name only*. Opening the explorer *at a specific transaction* would need the explorer to declare its own intent (for example `lez.explorer.open {tx|account|block}`), which is a small upstream change.

**What exists in our wallet (verified in code):**
- The engine builds explorer URLs itself (`service.rs` `open_explorer`), only for `lez:testnet` → `https://explorer.testnet.lez.logos.co/transaction/<hash>` or `/account/<id>`, rate-limited.
- "View in explorer" shows only on the send/proof result sheet (`ProofView.qml:168`) and only when the zone has an explorer. "View account in explorer" is on Receive; "Open in explorer" is on token detail. The faucet and testimonial apps have their own buttons.

**What's missing (why it doesn't feel like a normal wallet):**
1. **The detail sheet is too thin.** *Correction:* Home activity rows **do** open a sheet: `Home.qml:304` emits `openStatus`, and `qml/Main.qml:139-143` opens `ProofView`. It shows the outcome, "In block N", the fee (public native sends only), a short hash with copy, and "View in explorer". It lacks the date and time, from/to, amount and token, route, the requesting app and the full hash. History entries store no real timestamp for your own sends (`engine.rs:1837`) and no `kind` field (`TxRow.qml:19-23` guesses it from the title). Rows in **token detail** (`TokenDetail.qml:168`) don't open at all. References: Phantom "Sent" `e250c084`, Family "Received" `680c3a6e`, Glow failed `ac2866f9`.
2. The success toast and notification carry no link.
3. No explorer for local or custom zones, and no way to set one (a zone setting).
4. We never offer the Basecamp LEZ Explorer app. Possible: launch it by name, and ask upstream for an "open at transaction" intent (table above).
5. The links our engine builds (`/transaction/<lowercase hex>`, `/account/<base58>`) **match** the live explorer's own links (verified 2026-10-14 on block 5890's transactions). No change needed.
